# Enterprise HR Policies

## Section 1: Leave Management
### 1.1 Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues evenly at the rate of 1.66 days per month. Unused PTO up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year.

### 1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are granted 10 days of paid sick leave annually. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate may be required by the reporting manager.

### 1.3 Maternity & Paternity Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. Secondary caregivers (paternity leave) are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Section 2: Remote Work & Flexible Hours
### 2.1 Hybrid Work Policy
The company operates on a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in the office a minimum of 2 days per week (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays). Remote work days must be coordinated with team managers to ensure adequate coverage.

### 2.2 Core Working Hours
Core working hours are between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time. Employees must be available for meetings and collaboration during this window, though they have flexibility in how they complete their required 8-hour workday.

## Section 3: Performance & Upskilling
### 3.1 Performance Reviews
Performance reviews occur bi-annually (Q2 and Q4). The review focuses on KPI achievement (40%), Peer feedback (30%), and Skill development (30%). 

### 3.2 Professional Development Stipend
Each employee is allotted a $1,500 annual stipend for professional development. This can be used for certifications (e.g., AWS, PyTorch), conferences, or courses. Approval must be granted by the direct manager before booking.

## Section 4: Exit Procedures & Offboarding
### 4.1 Notice Period
The standard notice period for voluntary resignation is 4 weeks. For managerial roles and above, the notice period is 8 weeks.

### 4.2 Equipment Return
All company-issued equipment (laptops, monitors, access badges) must be returned to the IT department on or before the final day of employment. Failure to return equipment may result in deductions from the final paycheck.
